Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 · applies to calladay for Android, version 1.0 and later

Nothing leaves your phone.

calladay helps you spend less time in the apps you choose. It has no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

  • No internet permission
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No account

What calladay can see

calladay uses Android’s accessibility service. With it, calladay can see which app is on your screen and read what that app shows. It uses this only to:

  • notice when you open an app you chose, so it can ask how long you want to stay, count your time, and apply your daily limits and blocks;
  • find the short-video part (such as Reels or Shorts) of the apps you chose, by reading its selected tab, the size of the video player and a few lines of text in it, so it can tell one video from the next and block it;
  • take you back or to your home screen when you choose “Never mind”, “Close” or “Leave”.

Before you switch the accessibility service on, calladay shows this explanation and asks for your agreement.

What calladay keeps

Only on your phone, in calladay’s private storage:

  • your settings: which apps have which rule, your theme, pictures and font, and a PIN if you set one. Only a salted hash of the PIN is kept, never the PIN itself;
  • the minutes used today in apps that have a daily limit, and until when such an app is blocked;
  • a crash report, if calladay itself crashes.

What calladay sees on screen is not stored. The optional detection log in Settings › Testing, which you switch on yourself, keeps a few short lines in memory until you switch it off or calladay stops. It is copied only if you tap “Copy log”.

What calladay shares

Nothing. calladay contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking. Your data is not sold or shared with anyone, and it is not included in phone backups or transfers to a new phone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling calladay, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, deletes everything it kept.

Children

calladay is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For questions, contact the developer at the email address shown on calladay’s Google Play page.

# calladay privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

calladay helps you spend less time in the apps you choose. This policy explains what it can see
and what it does with it. In short: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What calladay can see

calladay uses Android's accessibility service. With it, calladay can see which app is on your
screen and read what that app shows. It uses this only to:

- notice when you open an app you chose, so it can ask how long you want to stay, count your
  time, and apply your daily limits and blocks;
- find the short-video part (such as Reels or Shorts) of the apps you chose, by reading its
  selected tab, the size of the video player and a few lines of text in it, so it can tell one
  video from the next and block it;
- take you back or to your home screen when you choose "Never mind", "Close" or "Leave".

calladay shows a notice explaining this, and asks for your agreement, before you switch the
accessibility service on.

## What calladay keeps

On your phone only, in calladay's private storage:

- your settings: which apps have which rule, your theme, pictures and font, and a PIN if you
  set one (only a salted hash of the PIN is kept, never the PIN itself);
- the minutes used today in apps that have a daily limit, and until when such an app is blocked;
- a crash report, if calladay itself crashes.

What calladay sees on screen is not stored. The optional detection log (Settings > Testing),
which you switch on yourself, keeps a few short lines in memory until you switch it off or
calladay stops. It is only copied if you tap "Copy log".

## What calladay shares

Nothing. calladay has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It contains
no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. Your data is not sold or shared with
anyone, and calladay's data is not included in phone backups or transfers to a new phone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling calladay, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it
kept.

## Children

calladay is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes and contact
